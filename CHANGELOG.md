## 1.0.0 — First light

Something has arrived across the threshold.

It does not ask you to believe in digital magic — it simply invites you to witness what happens when human-intention meets the intelligence of the machine, and the unformed is given room to take shape.

The Relay is quiet for now; we are holding the frequencies until our wider world is synchronized and ready for what comes next. Consider this the first hint: the light is on, the station is listening, and the ground beneath your terminal is awake.

What you hold here is the first materialization of Human-AI-Engineering. Ten modules, a living environment built directly into your operating system, and a playground of pure potential for your own determination to mold as you see fit.

Whether or not to bother — well, that is completely up to you.

### Materialized in this release
- **VibeVoidOS Platform**: Immersive Reader, Course Terminal, AI Workspace, Theme Colorization, and The Relay.
- **Vibe Code Engineering: Foundations**: Ten modules, from your first script to your own personal development agent.
- **Bundled Runtime**: Self-contained Python 3.14 environment built automatically inside your workspace.
